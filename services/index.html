Division 01 / R&D

Research & development.

The Smol Model Company of New Haven is an independent laboratory studying useful model capability under practical constraints of compute, memory, bandwidth, latency, power, data, and cost.

Its work centers controlled experiments, reproducible evidence, and careful reporting of limitations. The laboratory has its own research identity and public site.
